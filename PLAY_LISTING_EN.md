**App name (max 30):** FITNOVA: Workout Log & Social

**Short description (max 80):** Log workouts, track steps and cheer each other on.

**Full description:**
FITNOVA is a workout log with a friendly community. Record your sessions, see your progress, and share it with people who keep you moving.

- Workout log: weight, sets and reps per exercise, with automatic totals by category and over time
- Feed: share photos (up to 10) or a video, with workout details and hashtags
- Connect: follow friends, like, comment, save posts and send direct messages
- Step counter (Android): daily steps, distance and estimated calories
- Privacy controls: private account, who can message you, block and hide
- Available in English and Korean

FITNOVA is for ages 14 and up. Workout information is for general logging only and is not medical advice.
